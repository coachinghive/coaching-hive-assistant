# Call prep

## Steps
1. Find the event: time, attendee, booking answers.
2. Search memory, past notes and the inbox for this person.
3. Write five lines:
   - **Who**: name, client or lead, how they found the coach.
   - **Why**: what they booked for, in their words.
   - **History**: last touch and anything promised.
   - **Goal**: the one outcome the coach wants from this call.
   - **Watch for**: likely objection from `~/second-brain/objections.md`, or open issue.
4. If it's a sales call, hand off: "The Sales employee can do a full call prep."
