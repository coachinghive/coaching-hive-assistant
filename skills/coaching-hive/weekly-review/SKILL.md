# Weekly review

## Steps
1. Read `~/second-brain/kpis.md` for the targets.
2. Ask the coach for any numbers you can't see (one message, all at once):
   leads, conversations, calls booked, calls shown, sales, cash collected,
   clients active, clients at risk.
3. Show each number next to its target. Mark ✅ hit, ⚠️ close, ❌ missed.
4. Find the one bottleneck: the first step in the funnel that missed most.
5. Write: **Keep** (what worked), **Fix** (the bottleneck and one action),
   **Drop** (one thing that took time and didn't pay).
6. Offer to save the review to `~/second-brain/reviews/YYYY-WW.md`.

## Never
- Fill a missing number with a guess. Missing shows as `missing`, not 0.
